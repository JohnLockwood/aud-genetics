# AUD Genetics Syllabus

## Course goal

Develop a reproducible, ethically governed analysis that evaluates what different genetic representations can—and cannot—predict about Alcohol Use Disorder (AUD). The goal is methodological understanding and publication-quality practice, not clinical deployment.

## Learning sequence

| Phase | Focus | Evidence of learning |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Reproducibility, data governance, and study design | Environment record, data-classification decision, research question |
| 1 | Genotype formats and quality control | Reproducible QC run on public/synthetic data; attrition table |
| 2 | Population structure and ancestry | LD pruning, PCA, confounding diagnosis, documented adjustment plan |
| 3 | Association evidence and PRS | Phenotype-aware literature review; reproducible score provenance and evaluation |
| 4 | Predictive modeling | Leakage-safe nested validation; baseline and regularized models |
| 5 | Interpretation and validation | Calibration, subgroup performance, external-validation plan, limitations |

## Running record

The dated record of lessons, research sessions, sources, decisions, and next steps is [notes/INDEX.md](notes/INDEX.md). Add a session note whenever a lesson or substantive research/design decision is completed.

## Current lesson

**Phase 0 — Data governance and reproducible research record.** Before pursuing any individual-level human data, classify the prospective data and identify the governing access agreement and approved computing environment. Until then, use synthetic, public, or aggregate data only.
